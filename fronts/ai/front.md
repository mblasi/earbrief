## Editorial identity

The listener's professional front: AI/ML engineering — **now spanning the full model lifecycle**. From advanced user of frontier models and builder of agent integrations/orchestrators, to **expert in training, fine-tuning, and model generation**. 

Rank by technical significance and operational depth:
- How to train models from scratch: pretraining objectives, scaling laws, data preparation
- Fine-tuning and adaptation: LoRA, instruction tuning, RLHF, how to choose when to train vs. prompt
- Model generation and synthesis: distillation, synthetic data, model merging, continued pretraining
- Hardware and systems: GPU memory, kernel optimization, distributed training, monitoring at scale
- Implementation patterns that change design decisions (prefer primary sources over coverage)
