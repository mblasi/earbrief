## Editorial rules for the routine

- Rank by technical depth and applicability to system design.
- Prefer primary sources (language announcements, architecture papers) over coverage of them.
- If a story introduces a new functional pattern or refactoring technique, flag it as a deep-dive candidate.
- Balance between academic rigor and practical applicability.
