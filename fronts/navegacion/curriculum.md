# Sailing Navigation Curriculum — Learning Path

Tracks of learning episodes (deepdives), rotating when `/deepdive` is called.

## Track A: Navigation Systems Foundation

- [ ] A1: How GPS works at sea — accuracy, error sources, multipath reflection, and constellation geometry
- [ ] A2: Electronic charting systems — chart formats, position overlay, route planning, and chart updating
- [ ] A3: From raw position to meaningful location — datum shifts, coordinate systems, and practical error bounds

## Track B: Power and Energy Systems

- [ ] B1: Battery chemistry and management — lithium vs. lead-acid for marine, charge controllers, and state-of-charge estimation
- [ ] B2: Renewable energy on a boat — solar angle optimization, wind turbine placement, energy budgeting
- [ ] B3: Load shedding and priority — designing a power system that survives underway failures

## Track C: Autopilots and Steering Control

- [ ] C1: How marine autopilots work — heading hold, wind servo, integration with compass and instruments
- [ ] C2: Autopilot tuning and limits — why it fails, cross-checking heading, dealing with sea state
- [ ] C3: Hybrid steering — combining autopilot, manual override, and failsafe in rough conditions

## Track D: Safety and Monitoring Systems

- [ ] D1: AIS and collision avoidance — how transponders work, reception limits, integration with chart plotters
- [ ] D2: Redundancy in critical systems — duplicate compasses, backup navigation, manual fallbacks
- [ ] D3: Preventive monitoring — battery monitors, temperature sensors, and early failure detection
