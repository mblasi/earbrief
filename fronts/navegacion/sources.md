# Sailing Navigation Technology — Sources

Tier-1 (daily scan): Core sources for sailing tech, marine electronics, and practical seamanship.
Tier-2 (weekly scan): Deep dives, research, and specialized topics.

## Tier 1 (Daily scan)

- **Panbo** — Steve D'Antonio's marine electronics and systems blog, highly technical: autopilots, networking, solar, battery management, GPS integration, troubleshooting real-world failures. https://panbo.com
- **SeaBits** — Sailing technology newsletter, curated links on electronics, automation, safety systems, product reviews from actual sailors. https://seabits.substack.com
- **Open Boat Projects** — Open-source hardware for sailing: weather stations, AIS receivers, smart battery monitors, DIY electronics integrations. https://www.openbmp.org
- **Sailing Anarchy** (tech category) — Racing/cruising crossover; tracks innovation in autopilots, sails, navigation tech, boat systems. https://www.sailinganarchy.com
- **Practical Sailor** — Hands-on reviews of marine electronics, safety gear, power systems, diagnostics. https://www.practical-sailor.com
- **11 Knots** — Sailing data, navigation routing, weather integration, electronic charting, performance metrics. https://www.11knots.com

## Tier 2 (Weekly deep-dive sources)

- **SailGP** Technical Coverage — High-end foiling boat systems: control automation, power distribution, stability systems, sensor fusion (not race-focused, but pure tech). https://www.sailgp.com
- **Latitude 38** — West Coast sailing community, monthly tech roundup, marine electronics innovations, DIY solutions. https://www.latitude38.com
- **Cruising Association** — Seamanship, safety, systems redundancy, long-distance sailing tech and tactics. https://www.cruising.org.uk
- **Maritime Executive** (marine tech) — Commercial shipping and yachting tech trends, autonomous vessels, navigation systems evolution. https://www.maritime-executive.com
- **Electronics Weekly** (marine focus) — Sensor technology, wireless protocols, IoT for marine, battery/power innovations. https://www.electronicsweekly.com
- **Journal of Navigation** (Cambridge, behind paywall but some open access) — GPS accuracy, positioning algorithms, electronic charting standards. https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/journal-of-navigation
- **Boatyard Bulletin** — Maintenance, troubleshooting, system diagnostics, retrofit guides for older boats catching up to modern tech. https://www.boatyardbulletin.com

## Editorial rules

1. **Technology first**: Rank by how the system works and what it enables (reliability, automation, power efficiency). Skip regatta results unless they illustrate a systems breakthrough.
2. **Practical over exotic**: Prioritize tech that helps actual sailors navigate safely, maintain their boats, and make systems reliable for small crews or solo sailing. Skip luxury/racing-only features unless they're becoming mainstream.
3. **Real-world focus**: Prefer stories about sailors fixing, retrofitting, or integrating systems. Avoid hype about "smart boats" without substance.
4. **Safety is core**: Redundancy, failure modes, detection systems, and recovery strategies are always relevant to this front.
5. **DIY and open**: Give high rank to open-source hardware projects, Arduino/Raspberry Pi integrations, and community-shared solutions — they empower sailors to understand and maintain their systems.
