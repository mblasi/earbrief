## Editorial identity

The enthusiast's perspective on sailing technology and navigation systems. Not focused on racing or professional leagues, but on the practical technology enabling safe and efficient sailing: **automation, power management, navigation safety, marine electronics, and sailing systems**.

Technical focus on:
- Navigation systems: GPS, autopilot, chart plotting, electronic charts, positioning accuracy
- Power and energy: battery management, solar/wind charging, power budgeting for cruising
- Safety and monitoring: AIS, weather routing, system redundancy, failure detection and recovery
- Automation and control: sail trim automation, engine management, integrated boat systems
- Marine electronics: sensor networks, integration patterns, wireless comms at sea
- Practical seamanship: technology that makes solo/small-crew sailing safer and more practical

Audience: Sailors who want to understand the tech behind modern boats, system reliability, and how to maintain/troubleshoot underway.
